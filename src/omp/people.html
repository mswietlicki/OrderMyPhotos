Give each person a full name and a nickname: album names use the nickname, or the first name, and photo titles use the names. One person split over several cards: select them and Merge; the same full name on two cards merges them too. "This is me" marks you; "I don't know them" keeps a stranger out of the names. A card you leave without a name is still someone you know: their photos are kept like a named person's. Cards marked "I don't know them" or "Not a person" go to Hidden, where you can undo it.
